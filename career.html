Career path

Developing skills at every stage.

Career development does not stop when an employee finishes an apprenticeship or earns a license. The training changes as the employee’s role changes. The purpose stays the same: continue developing the knowledge, skills, and leadership needed for the next level.

1

Electrical apprentice

Learn the trade in class, in the lab, and on the job.

2

Journeyman electrician

Build license-ready knowledge and independent skill.

3

Leadman

Take on more responsibility with the crew.

4

Foreman

Plan the day, coordinate people, and protect quality.

5

Site superintendent

Lead the job and develop the people doing the work.

How training follows the work

The training changes as the role changes.

A field career may progress through apprentice, journeyman, leadman, foreman, and site superintendent. Electrical experience can also create opportunities in BIM, project management, electrical engineering, estimating, sales, and other areas of the business.

5K Training & Development supports that progression with apprenticeship, licensing preparation, project management certification, leadership development, and foreman and superintendent training.
