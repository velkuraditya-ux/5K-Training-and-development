What we offer

Programs for every stage of the work.

Electrical construction requires technical knowledge, sound judgment, safety awareness, and strong leadership in the field. 5K Training & Development gives employees a structured way to keep developing throughout their careers.

Primary program

5K Electrical Apprenticeship Program

A four-year, earn-while-you-learn pathway for eligible employees. Registered with the U.S. Department of Labor and based on the NCCER curriculum, the program combines classroom instruction, hands-on lab work, real-world jobsite experience, and mentorship from experienced journeyman electricians.

Apprentices progress through electrical foundations, core skills, applied practice, and advanced skills and leadership. Training includes electrical theory, National Electrical Code, wiring methods, safety, conduit bending, blueprint reading, and troubleshooting.

Journeyman exam preparation

27.5% Texas Journeyman Electrician exam statewide pass rate in fiscal year 2025.

Licensing is an important step in an electrician’s career, and the journeyman exam can be challenging. 5K T&D provides focused preparation for employees working toward their journeyman license, with a structured plan to review electrical concepts and strengthen code knowledge.

NCCER project management certification

Moving into project leadership requires a broader understanding of how electrical work is planned, coordinated, and managed. Training supports the knowledge needed to manage costs, schedules, contracts, teams, and project execution.

Leadership development

Strong field leaders need technical experience, and they also need to communicate clearly, plan ahead, make decisions, and lead people. Training focuses on communication, accountability, problem-solving, planning, and employee development that employees can apply on the job.

Foreman & superintendent training

Training focused on the day-to-day responsibilities of field leadership, including crew coordination, staffing, tools and materials, safety, productivity, quality, problem-solving, and daily operations. Leaders also strengthen the skills needed to support customers and develop the people on their teams.
