About

Training that supports a career in the electrical trade.

5K Training & Development is the workforce education branch of the 5K Holdings family, purpose-built to develop the next generation of skilled electricians. 5K T&D provides structured training and development for employees at different stages of their electrical careers.

Programs support technical skill development, licensing preparation, project management, field leadership, and career growth. Training combines classroom instruction, hands-on learning, skills assessment, exam preparation, and leadership development with the experience employees gain on the job.
